Privacy

Last updated 7 October 2026

This page covers fysh.site itself: the front page and the pages linked from its footer. Each tool has its own privacy page that goes into detail about that tool:

  • Event Horizon Web privacy
  • Red Rocket privacy
  • Warp Gate privacy

Who runs this

fysh.site is run by one person as a personal project, not by a company. Questions about this page or about data held about you go to privacy@fysh.site, which is read by the person who runs the site.

What this site records

fysh.site has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms, no analytics and no tracking scripts of its own. It sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser.

The site is served through Cloudflare, which handles every request on the way in. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request details (the page asked for, your browser type) to deliver the page and block attacks. That's covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Advertising and cookies

The front page may show ads from Google AdSense. When it does, your browser loads Google's ad script directly from Google, so Google sees your IP address and may set cookies or read device identifiers to choose and measure ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this site and other sites.
  • Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses this data is explained in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message first, with options to consent, refuse or manage choices. If you refuse, any ads shown are non-personalised. This site sends Google nothing about you beyond what your browser sends when it loads the script.

Links to other sites

The front page links to GitHub, Ko-fi and the Fysh tools. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and nothing about your visit here is passed to them except the usual referring page your browser may send.

If you choose to tip with Monero or Bitcoin, the front page shows you an address to copy. Nothing is sent anywhere when you do that. Keep in mind that Bitcoin transactions are public on the blockchain.

Children

This site isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

Your rights

Since fysh.site keeps no personal data of its own, there's normally nothing here to access or delete. If you think something about you is held, write to privacy@fysh.site and I'll check and answer. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California) you also have rights to access, correct and delete your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
